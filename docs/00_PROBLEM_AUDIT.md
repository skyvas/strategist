# 00: Problem Audit & Business Context

> **Author**: Business Discovery & Requirements Analyst  
> **Status**: In Discovery (Phase 1 & 2: Root Problem & Actor Mapping)  
> **Project**: Import/Export Pipeline (India to Canada only)

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Objectives
- **Target Problem**: Establishing a new import business pipeline from India to Canada targeting grocery stores serving new immigrants.
- **Current State**: New business venture. Two partners: 'H' (based in India) and 'A' (based in Canada). Need to define job descriptions, delegation of tasks, and an automated pipeline to deliver the solution.
- **Goal**: Build the operational pipeline and delegate responsibilities effectively using AI, while avoiding cost overruns, fatigue, and building a theoretical solution that fails in the real world.

---

## 2. Actor & Workflow Mapping (WIP)
- **Actor 1**: Partner 'H' (Location: India) - *Sourcing, Vendor QA, Indian export compliance (FSSAI/APEDA).*
- **Actor 2**: Partner 'A' (Location: Canada) - *Import compliance (CFIA/SFC), customs brokerage, and B2B sales.*
- **System/AI Actor**: Orchestrator for the pipeline - *Document validation, compliance checks, restock triggers.*

---

## 3. Product Scope & Cost of Doing Nothing
- **Product Focus**: Non-perishable foods and consumables (Spices, RTE meals, Staples). *Note: Perishables are currently excluded to minimize logistical overhead and cold-chain risks.*
- **Risks Avoided**: Manual documentation errors leading to customs seizures, time-zone fatigue between partners, and out-of-stock scenarios.

---

## 4. Scope & Anti-Goals
- **In Scope**:
  - Research phase for India -> Canada import pipeline.
  - Job description and delegation mapping for H and A.
  - Product ideation for grocery store B2B sales.
- **Anti-Goals**:
  - Perishable goods requiring complex cold-chain logistics.
  - Over-engineered software solutions (prefer headless/messaging integration).
