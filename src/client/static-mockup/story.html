The Heritage & Sourcing

The Indian Condiment. Reimagined.

For decades, the Indian diaspora and global food explorers have settled for mass-produced supermarket jars packed with excessive sodium, stale oils, and artificial preservatives. We started Kairi & Co. to bring true, uncompromised Indian culinary craft to the Canadian table.

The Raw Ingredient

Why "Kairi"?

In Hindi, Kairi translates to crisp, tart raw green mango—the undisputed crown jewel of Indian pickling culture. Before it softens into sweetness, the green mango holds an intensely bright acidity that cuts right through rich spices.

Every summer across India, courtyards fill with ceramic jars as grandmothers hand-slice bushels of kairi, rub them with coarse sea salt and freshly cracked turmeric, and set them out under the blazing sun. That deep ritual of patience and seasonal rhythm is what we capture in every single jar.

Ancient Technique

The Soul of the Barni (Sun-Curing)

Industrial food brands rush the process by boiling unripe produce in diluted acetic acid (vinegar) and artificial colorings. The result? Mushy fruit floating in greasy, separated brine.

At Kairi & Co., we refuse to take shortcuts. We sun-cure our achar for 21 days in traditional glazed ceramic barnis under natural warmth. This slow, biological fermentation allows cold-pressed, pungent mustard oil and roasted methi (fenugreek) seeds to naturally cure the fruit skins, creating a firm bite and a flavor depth that only patience can build.
