Ingredients & Allergen Info +

Ingredients: Hand-cut Green Mango (65%), Cold-Pressed Pure Mustard Oil, Iodized Salt, Red Chilli Powder, Fenugreek Seeds (Methi), Fennel Seeds (Saunf), Nigella Seeds (Kalonji), Turmeric Powder, Asafoetida (Hing), Whole Spices.

Allergens: Contains Mustard. Free from artificial preservatives, MSG, synthetic food colors, or added corn syrups. 100% Plant-Based & Naturally Gluten-Free.

The Traditional Craft & Sun-Curing +

Unlike commercial supermarket pickles that are quick-boiled with acetic acid, Kairi & Co. follows the ancient *Dhoop Vidhi* (sun-curing). The freshly cut raw green mangoes are salted and sun-cured in traditional ceramic barnis under natural warmth for 21 days.

This allows the natural probiotic lactobacillus cultures to develop, softening the mango skins while preserving a vibrant crunch, fully infused with cold-pressed mustard oil.

Pairing Suggestions & Kitchen Ideas +

Traditional: Essential alongside hot aloo parathas with homemade butter, dal tadka with steamed basmati, or yellow moong khichdi.

Modern Reimagined: Smear inside a gourmet sourdough grilled cheese, dollop over morning avocado toast with runny eggs, or mix into Greek yogurt for a smoky-spicy fry dip.

Shipping, Storage & Shelf Life +

Canadian Shipping: All orders are carefully packed in recyclable molded pulp cushioning and dispatched from our Toronto warehouse via Canada Post Expedited Parcel (2-4 business days). Orders over $50 CAD ship FREE.

Shelf Life: 18 months unopened. Once opened, always use a clean, dry spoon. Ensure mango pieces remain submerged under the natural spice oil layer. Store in a cool pantry or refrigerate.
